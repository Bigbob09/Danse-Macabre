Void-bound

Episkopos

Reality tares to the claws of that armoured beast as it pokes it's eye through, searching for anything it can grab. It's fingers are unnaturally geometric and freakishly long. The one eye it always pokes through has a deep purple iris that is large enough to fit a human inside if fully contracted.

Void-bound, or referred to as “Episkopos” after the “People's enlightenment act”, was seemingly manifested through those who didn't act during the “Fallout uprising” or other movement events. This was determined when Void-bound's rift fragments were found in the houses of those who reportedly did not attend those events. Many theories lead to Void-bound targeting people that don't take action for their passions, according to an inscription marked next to the locations of its victims that say “Choosing the easy way rather than the right way could slowly set your mind up for failure, a slow cruise to an ever coming downfall”
