Petunias

Before the awakening of her ability, she was the caretaker to a garden centre and was very passionate about her job. At home she had a loving husband that worked in national infrastructure. Once Sin was unleashed, Her husband died protecting Petunias from the manifestations. She watched him get torn in half close enough to feel the warmth of his blood splatter against her face, traumatising her in the instant. But she couldn't grieve and had to keep running. One day and twenty hours after the “crusade of unholy hatred” when she went back the garden centre she worked at and began tending to the flowers again, causing her awakening through the overwhelming reminiscence of what she had lost.

Petunias' Ability:

Petunias' ability “Floral Resonance” allows Petunias to influence, grow, and share the senses of any and all floral life. It is also noted that she has one of the highest energy reserves and output of any awakened human. After creating and expanding their garden, she funnelled her energy into the surrounding plants to create a barrier of pure positive energy that can repel any manifestations (including Sin) as long as she can maintain her energy output.

Throughout the course of her life as an awakened human, she maintained her barrier and remained peaceful to any who entered seeking shelter.

Petunias' death and rebirth:

People kept entering her barrier, predominantly the same nine people, but they never talked to Petunias or acknowledged that the barrier was hers. This lead to Petunias suspecting that they are using and taking advantage of her. However, she never confronted them during her lifetime. One day, six of the nine people from before entered her barrier, however, they were being chased by more manifestations than petunias could handle. The manifestations shattered the barrier, consuming most of Petunias energy, preventing her from running. Her final words before her death, were “All I wanted was to help, not the using I receive”. Her autopsy reported a fatal gash on the left side of her lower ribs, and no signs of struggle or resistance in her death.

One day later, after the reports of her death, A medically trained professional came to check on her and see if she can be helped. However, when they arrived, Petunias' body was firmly rooted to the ground, and there was no perceivable heartbeat. When he began to leave the area, he turned back to notice a large vine sprout from the ground, The vine was purple, and reached down until it wrapped around Petunias' chest. It lifted her until she was at her feet, causing the reincarnated Petunias to stumble at her feet, grabbing at the vine for support as she found her footing. As she found her feet and stood upright, the vine quickly withered away and a symbol was etched into the ground around her. Still looking down she uttered to herself “Rise, a requiem stays never forgotten, in this oh so dark world”. She looked up at the man staring at her in shock and said “Hey, you there. That symbol. You tried to help me. My gratitudes to you, you won't go forgotten, but I must leave”. Before turning away from him and walking into the forest of her garden.
