Apostate

Before the awakening of their ability, James Fangrino, commonly known as “Apostate”, was a normal boy at the age of seventeen. He always studied hard and tried his best in school, being most interested in the physics of light and refraction. He always stayed faithful to the beliefs of treating others well in alignment to the teachings of the Archangel Raphael. However, this would lead him to be bullied constantly in school. One day, after the “crusade of unholy hatred”, James was being bullied in school, he was surrounded by three bullies while pinned against a wall. Suddenly, a shimmer of light appeared through the clouds, and he awakened. Unknowing of how to control his powers yet, he expelled a light formation in a sea urchin like structure surrounding his body. This immediately fatally wounded two of his bullies, piercing one thought the heart and decapitating the other. The final bully was mortally wounded, getting impaled on the shoulder and tearing his arm off, he soon died of blood loss and shock before any medical aid could arrive. Overcome with guilt and panic, James ran away into the forest where he resides.

Apostate's ability:

Apostate's ability “malignant lamp” allows him to create reflections and refractions in the surrounding light around him, causing the light rays to brighten into visible beams that act as physical objects, at the cost of them being restricted to subsonic speeds. This ability is either activated with hand signals that he can influence with enough practice, or chants. He can also convert his energy into light that gets expelled from his body. Common uses for this ability include, creating beams of light, creating weapons to through with the light, reinforcing parts of his body with light, expelling pure light energy from his body to repel people away from him, propelling himself into the air using light.

Apostate's eternal war:

When Apostate ran into the forest, many reports were released about him. This led investigators to be sent out to question him. Everyone sent was killed. Later, a bounty was placed on him. Hundreds of assassins and awakened humans attempted to claim the bounty, but none were successful. This caused the forest surrounding him to become a desolate wasteland where whoever enters is deemed to challenge him and pay with their life, deeming him the name “Apostate”.
