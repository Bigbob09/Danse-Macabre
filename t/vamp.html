Vamp

Bethany

Waves of compulsion screech from it's gaping mouth. It resembles and angler fish that swims through the air. It excretes a black oily mucus, resembling tar, from it's skin. It has a single eye that hangs from it's head mounted antenna. The creature's mouth spans a quarter round it's body and is filled with two rows of jagged teeth.

Vamp, or refereed to as “Bethany” after the “People's enlightenment act”, was seemingly manifested through the death of the famous corrupt politician and dictator, Kionon Niokavich, after the “Fallout uprising”. Sin seemingly used Niokavich's immense following and influence to alter his form into Vamp. From observations on unfortunate others, it seems that Vamp has the ability to control or influence people and other animals through it's series of screeches and melodies. The lures on record are as follows. “Call of the Deep” - forces the listener to get as low as possible and not move until the melody is finished. “The sweet hail to me” - forces to listener to get as close to Vamp as possible. “Song of Icy sheer” - forces the listener to stop moving to the best of their ability for ten seconds, it is noticed that when a person is in this state they are overcome with a cold sensation.
