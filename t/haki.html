Hara-Kiri

Cupcake

Countless rows of scaled mass spanned it's entire horrific length. It's head bares a feline like structure with alterations such as only having one eye socket despite using three eyes, having what is deemed to be gills, it's mouth consisting of three main spikes, one larger spike on the roof of it's mouth and two smaller on the bottom. It's body consists of 28 sections, each section being made of a hard exoskeleton and having flexible joints at either side. Each section has three wings, evenly spaced around it. The purpose of these wings are unknown.

Hara-kiri, or commonly refereed to as “Cupcake” after the “People's Enlightenment act”, was seemingly manifested through the suicide of humans. Sin seemingly used the immense negative release of the act of suicide to create each section of the creature. This was determined when comparing observations with reported suicides when they were directly proportional. It is also noted that after its manifestation each suicide victim found had a specific strain of floral life growing out of and protecting their body that we deem the flor de muerto.

Flor de muerto, or the flower of the dead, is a flora that was found on the victims of suicide. It is mostly gray with it's stem having a bark like consistency while being soft to the touch. It's flower has many buds that converge in the centre, being a similar colour to the stem but slightly darker in shade. Many people that have encountered and asked to describe the flower have compared it to a Marigold. However, we have not encountered any Marigold species since the culling. Flor de muerto seemingly absorbs energy from the host body, as observations have found it still thriving in areas of low light level. Subjects that have been exposed to Flor de muerto through physical contact have described the experience as reliving their “lowest moments” or times of loss. This contact sent many subjects into depressive states nearing suicide, this could be the objective of the flora or how it reproduces as from inspection no ovaries were found on it's anatomy. Until further notice any contact is forbidden unless authorised and monitored.
